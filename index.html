      ⚙️ What happens next

      1 Your document is OCR-scanned and your land details (owner, khasra, area...) are auto-read.
      2 It's placed in the Revenue Officer's queue with a fixed deadline for physical inspection.
      3 The officer visits / calls to confirm ownership, area, and boundaries in person.
      4 Once confirmed, it's marked Verified with the officer's name — visible to you under My Submissions.
      5 If two people claim the same land, both are held for verification — no submission is fast-tracked over another.
      6 If the deadline is missed, your case is automatically escalated to a senior authority.
